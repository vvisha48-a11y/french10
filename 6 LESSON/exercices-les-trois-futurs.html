🟣 Futur Antérieur (Earlier Action) CBSE 2019
Dès que nous ___ nos examens, nous irons en France.
"As soon as we finish / have finished our exams, we will go to France."
👇 Click the correct conjugated verb:
🧠 Mental Trigger Flowchart: Chronological Future Order
Step 1: Clue Spotted
🔍 "Dès que" + "irons"
Notice conjunction Dès que + main verb irons (Futur Simple).
Step 2: Timeline Flow
⏱️ Action 1 ➔ Action 2
Exams finish FIRST (Action 1) ➔ Trip to France SECOND (Action 2).
Step 3: Math Formula
📐 Avoir (FS) + P.P.
nous + aurons + fini = aurons fini.
Step 4: Answer
✅ aurons fini
Tense: Futur Antérieur.
⚡

Golden Law (Short & Crisp)

After Quand / Dès que / Aussitôt que, the earlier completed future action MUST take Futur Antérieur.

✅ Why "aurons fini"?
• Action 1 happens before we go to France in the future.
• Conjunction Dès que strictly triggers Futur Antérieur here.
❌ Why NOT "finirons"?
• finirons is Futur Simple. We cannot use two independent simultaneous future actions when one is completed first.
🔵 Futur Simple (Time Marker) CBSE 2018
Demain, mes parents ___ pour le Canada.
"Tomorrow, my parents will leave for Canada."
👇 Click the correct conjugated verb:
🧠 Mental Trigger Flowchart: Single Calendar Marker
Step 1: Clue Spotted
🔍 "Demain"
Sentence opens with time marker Demain (Tomorrow). Single clause.
Step 2: Timeline Flow
⏱️ Scheduled Future
Standard planned future departure occurring tomorrow. No preceding action.
Step 3: Math Formula
📐 Infinitive + -ont
mes parents (ils) + partir + -ont = partiront.
Step 4: Answer
✅ partiront
Tense: Futur Simple.
⚡

Golden Law (Short & Crisp)

Key future time markers like demain, l'année prochaine, plus tard require standard Futur Simple.

✅ Why "partiront"?
• Standard Future Simple conjugation for 3rd person plural (ils / mes parents).
❌ Why NOT "seront partis"?
• Futur Antérieur requires a second future reference clause (e.g. Quand...). There is none here.
🟢 Futur Proche (Imminent Action) CBSE 2020
Le train ___ dans cinq minutes, dépêche-toi !
"The train is going to arrive in five minutes, hurry up!"
👇 Click the correct conjugated verb:
🧠 Mental Trigger Flowchart: Urgency & Immediate Arrival
Step 1: Clue Spotted
🔍 "dans 5 min" + "dépêche-toi!"
Short countdown (dans cinq minutes) + imperative exclamation (dépêche-toi !).
Step 2: Timeline Flow
⏱️ Imminent (Minutes Away)
Happening right now, virtually immediate. Signals Futur Proche.
Step 3: Math Formula
📐 Aller (Présent) + Inf
le train (il) + va + arriver = va arriver.
Step 4: Answer
✅ va arriver
Tense: Futur Proche.
⚡

Golden Law (Short & Crisp)

Short countdowns (dans 5 minutes) paired with imperative urgency (dépêche-toi !) trigger Futur Proche.

✅ Why "va arriver"?
• Expresses imminent, virtually occurring reality right in front of us.
❌ Why NOT "arrivera"?
• arrivera sounds like distant, detached prediction rather than the immediate urgency demanded by dépêche-toi !
🔵 Futur Simple (Result Clause) CBSE 2017
Quand tu auras lu ce roman, tu me le ___.
"When you have read this novel, you will return it to me."
👇 Click the correct conjugated verb:
🧠 Mental Trigger Flowchart: Complementary Future Clause
Step 1: Clue Spotted
🔍 "Quand" + "auras lu"
Clause 1 already has auras lu (Futur Antérieur)!
Step 2: Timeline Flow
⏱️ Action 1 already set!
Read novel = Action 1 (FA). Returning it = Action 2 (Later Future).
Step 3: Math Formula
📐 Drop -e + -as
rendre ➔ drop final 'e' (rendr-) + -as = rendras.
Step 4: Answer
✅ rendras
Tense: Futur Simple.
⚡

Golden Law (Short & Crisp)

In a Quand sentence: One clause is Futur Antérieur (Action 1) ➔ The other clause MUST be Futur Simple (Action 2)!

✅ Why "rendras"?
• Pairs perfectly with auras lu to complete the Action 1 / Action 2 sequence.
❌ Why NOT "auras rendu"?
• Never put Futur Antérieur in both clauses! One must be Futur Simple.
🟣 Futur Antérieur (Conjunction Clause) CBSE 2016
Aussitôt qu'il ___ son premier salaire, il achètera un ordinateur.
"As soon as he receives / has received his first salary, he will buy a computer."
👇 Click the correct conjugated verb:
🧠 Mental Trigger Flowchart: Aussitôt que Trigger
Step 1: Clue Spotted
🔍 "Aussitôt qu'" + "achètera"
Conjunction Aussitôt que (as soon as) + Clause 2 is achètera (Futur Simple).
Step 2: Timeline Flow
⏱️ Salary First ➔ Buy Second
He must receive the money FIRST (Action 1) before buying the PC (Action 2).
Step 3: Math Formula
📐 Avoir (FS) + reçu
il + aura + reçu (P.P. of recevoir) = aura reçu.
Step 4: Answer
✅ aura reçu
Tense: Futur Antérieur.
⚡

Golden Law (Short & Crisp)

The clause attached directly to Aussitôt que represents Action 1 and takes Futur Antérieur.

✅ Why "aura reçu"?
• Accurately establishes that receiving the salary must finish prior to purchasing.
❌ Why NOT "recevra"?
• recevra fails to establish that receiving the salary is completed first.

Direct physical observation in the immediate present (Regardez...).

Rain is about to pour down in seconds. 100% certainty = Futur Proche.

❌ Why NOT "pleuvra"?
• pleuvra is a distant weather forecast, not the imminent event happening right now.
🔵 Futur Simple (Distant Marker) CBSE 2022
L'année prochaine, vous ___ vos études à l'étranger.
"Next year, you will do your studies abroad."
👇 Click the correct conjugated verb:
🧠 Mental Trigger Flowchart: Distant Future Time Marker
Step 1: Clue Spotted
🔍 "L'année prochaine"
Time indicator L'année prochaine (Next year). Long-term future.
Step 2: Timeline Flow
⏱️ Months Ahead
Distant calendar event. Not immediate. Standard Futur Simple.
Step 3: Math Formula
📐 Stem fer- + -ez
Irregular stem of faire is fer- + -ez (vous) = ferez.
Step 4: Answer
✅ ferez

Golden Law (Short & Crisp)

Distant dates like l'année prochaine / le mois prochain take Futur Simple with the irregular stem fer-.

✅ Why "ferez"?
• Correct future stem (fer-) + -ez ending for vous.
❌ Why NOT "allez faire"?
• L'année prochaine is 12 months away — far too distant for Futur Proche!
🟣 Futur Antérieur (Être + Agreement) CBSE 2023
Dès qu'elles ___ de l'école, elles regarderont la télévision.
"As soon as they return / have returned from school, they will watch television."
👇 Click the correct conjugated verb:
🧠 Mental Trigger Flowchart: Être Verb & Feminine Plural Agreement
Step 1: Clue Spotted
🔍 "Dès qu'" + "rentrer"
Dès que + regarderont (FS) + verb rentrer is a DR MRS VANDERTRAMP verb!
Step 2: Timeline Flow
⏱️ Return Home ➔ Watch TV
Returning home is Action 1 = Futur Antérieur.
Step 3: Math Formula
📐 Être (FS) + rentré + es
elles ➔ seront + rentré + -es = seront rentrées.
Step 4: Answer
✅ seront rentrées
Tense: Futur Antérieur (Feminine Plural Agreement!).
⚡

Golden Law (Short & Crisp)

Verbs of movement (rentrer, arriver, partir...) take ÊTRE in Futur Antérieur and MUST agree with subject (elles = +es)!

✅ Why "seront rentrées"?
• Correct auxiliary ÊTRE in FS (seront) + agreement with elles (-es).
❌ Why NOT "seront rentré"?
• Trap! Missing the mandatory feminine plural agreement -es will lose CBSE exam marks!
🟣 Futur Antérieur (Reversed Clause Order) CBSE 2018
Je vous téléphonerai quand je ___ une bonne solution.
"I will phone you when I have found a good solution."
👇 Click the correct conjugated verb:
🧠 Mental Trigger Flowchart: "quand" in the Second Clause
Step 1: Clue Spotted
🔍 "téléphonerai" + "quand"
Main clause is téléphonerai (Futur Simple). Conjunction quand starts clause 2.
Step 2: Timeline Flow
⏱️ Find First ➔ Call Second
Logic check: I must find the solution FIRST (Action 1) before phoning you!
Step 3: Math Formula
📐 Avoir (je) + trouvé
je + aurai + trouvé (P.P. of trouver) = aurai trouvé.
Step 4: Answer
✅ aurai trouvé
Tense: Futur Antérieur.
⚡

Golden Law (Short & Crisp)

Even if quand is placed in the 2nd half of the sentence, the clause with quand remains Action 1 = Futur Antérieur!

✅ Why "aurai trouvé"?
• Finding the solution precedes the phone call chronologically in the future.
❌ Why NOT "trouverai"?
• Would imply both actions happen at the exact same moment without prerequisite completion.

🔍 "Une fois que" + "auras terminé"
Clause 1 is already in auras terminé (Futur Antérieur)!
Step 2: Timeline Flow
⏱️ Action 2 (Later Consequence)
Since Action 1 is already settled, the main result clause MUST be Futur Simple.

When the condition clause has Une fois que + Futur Antérieur, the main result clause is always Futur Simple.

✅ Why "pourras"?
• Futur Simple irregular stem pourr- + -as ending for tu.
❌ Why NOT "auras pu"?
• Never duplicate Futur Antérieur in both clauses! One must be Futur Simple.

🟣 Futur Antérieur (Être Movement Verb) CBSE 2016
Dès que le professeur ___ dans la classe, les élèves se lèveront.
"As soon as the teacher has entered the classroom, the students will stand up."
👇 Click the correct conjugated verb:
🧠 Mental Trigger Flowchart: Movement Verb with Être
Step 1: Clue Spotted
🔍 "Dès que" + "se lèveront"
Dès que + se lèveront (FS) + verb entrer takes ÊTRE!
Step 2: Timeline Flow
⏱️ Teacher Enters ➔ Students Rise
Entering is completed FIRST (Action 1) before students stand up (Action 2).
Step 3: Math Formula
📐 Être (il) + entré
le professeur (il) + sera + entré = sera entré.
Step 4: Answer
✅ sera entré
Tense: Futur Antérieur.
⚡

Golden Law (Short & Crisp)

Action 1 with Dès que requires Futur Antérieur. Entrer takes auxiliary ÊTRE (sera entré), NEVER avoir!

✅ Why "sera entré"?
• Uses the correct auxiliary verb Être for the movement verb entrer.
❌ Why NOT "aura entré"?
• Big grammatical error! entrer is conjugated with être, never with avoir.

🟣 Futur Antérieur (Prerequisite Condition) CBSE 2023
Quand nous ___ nos valises, nous appellerons un taxi pour l'aéroport.
"When we have packed our suitcases, we will call a taxi for the airport."
👇 Click the correct conjugated verb:
🧠 Mental Trigger Flowchart: Chronological Prerequisite Sequence
Step 1: Clue Spotted
🔍 "Quand" + "appellerons"
Conjunction Quand + Clause 2 has appellerons (Futur Simple).
Step 2: Timeline Flow
⏱️ Pack First ➔ Call Taxi Second
You pack luggage FIRST (Action 1) before calling the taxi to the airport.
Step 3: Math Formula
📐 Avoir (FS) + fait
nous + aurons + fait (P.P. of faire) = aurons fait.
Step 4: Answer
✅ aurons fait

Golden Law (Short & Crisp)

With Quand + Futur Simple in the main clause, the prerequisite completed action is always Futur Antérieur.

✅ Why "aurons fait"?
• Correct auxiliary aurons + past participle fait for the prerequisite action.
❌ Why NOT "ferons"?
• ferons is Futur Simple. We cannot use two independent simultaneous future actions here.
