## HOW TO USE THE DIFFERENT TENSES IN FRENCH ?
1. 3 types of verbs :
- First group :
The first group of verbs includes all the verbs ending in ER. They are also the most regular and the
easier to conjugate.
- Second group :
The second group of verbs includes all the verbs ending in IR. They are a bit more complicated to
conjugate but fairly regular.
- Third group :
The third group of verbs is the most irregular and basically includes all the verbs that don't fit in the first
and second group.
2. What part of the verb do we conjugate ?
- First group :
The ending of the first group of verbs is ER, this part called will be conjugate when the stem (le radical)
stays the same.
Example : Changer (to change)
"Chang" is the stem, and won't change
- Second group :
The ending of the first group of verbs is IR, this part called will be conjugate when the stem (le radical)
stays the same.
Example : Finir (to finish)
"Fin" is the stem, and won't change
- Third group :
This group doesn't follow any rules unfortunately. There are a lot of different types of verbs in this group
and we will see them gradually in this book.

## UNDERSTAND FRENCH TENSES
Here you will find the equivalent of the tenses we will see in English.
Important note : I purposely did not include some of the tenses listed at the end of this page. I found them more
confusing than anything as we almost never use them. I always tell my students than it would be good to look at them
when they reach an advanced level. Before that, studying even more complicated conjugation is not very useful in my
opinion.
1. Infinitif présent (infinitive present)
It is the bare tense of the verb Changer - To change
2. Infinitif passé (infinitive past)
This one is composed of the auxiliary in the infinitive form followed by the past participle (participe passé)
Avoir mangé - To have eaten
3. Participe présent (participe present)
Simply the equivalent of ING Changeant - Changing
4. Participe passé (past participle)
The equivalent of the past participle Changé - Changed
5. Présent (present)
Je change - I am changing / I change
6. Passé composé
J'ai changé - I changed
7. Imparfait
Je changeais - I was changing
8. Plus-que-parfait
J'avais changé - I had changed
9. Futur simple
Je changerai - I will change
10. Futur antérieur
J'aurai changé - I will have changed
11. Conditionnel présent
Je changerais - I would change
12. Conditionnel passé
J'aurais changé - I would have changed
13. Subjonctif présent
Que je mange - That I eat
14. Impératif présent
Mange ! - Eat !
NOT INCLUDED TENSES :
Passé simple - Passé antérieur - Futur antérieur - Subjonctif passé - Subjonctif imparfait - Subjonctif plus-
que-parfait - Impératif passé
