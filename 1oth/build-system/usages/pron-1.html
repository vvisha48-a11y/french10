1a Usages · La routine quotidienne
Usage 1 sur 2

L'action revient sur le sujet

The subject does the action to itself. Almost every daily-routine verb in French is pronominal.

Je me réveille à sept heures, puis je m'habille.

I wake up at seven, then I get dressed.

Compare — the same verb without the pronoun changes meaning:

Je lave la voiture. ← I wash the car
Je me lave. ← I wash myself

The pronoun is what sends the action back to the subject.

1b Usages · Le sens réciproque
Usage 2 sur 2

L'un l'autre — et les verbes qui changent de sens

Le réciproque — two or more people doing it to each other. Only possible in the plural.

Nous nous écrivons souvent.

We write to each other often.

Verbs whose meaning shifts entirely:

aller = to go → s'en aller = to leave
demander = to ask → se demander = to wonder
rendre = to give back → se rendre compte = to realise

Learn these as separate vocabulary items, not as variants.
