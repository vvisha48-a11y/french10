Quand deux pronoms se rencontrent

Two pronouns in one sentence follow a fixed order. You cannot swap them, and you cannot separate them from the verb.

Il donne le livre à Marie. → Il le lui donne.

He gives it to her. — slot 2 then slot 3.

⚠️ L'exception de l'impératif affirmatif. There the pronouns move behind the verb, joined by hyphens, and me becomes moi.

Donne-le-moi !  ·  but: Ne me le donne pas !

Negative imperative goes straight back to the normal order.
