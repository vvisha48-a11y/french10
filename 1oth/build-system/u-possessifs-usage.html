2 Mémo visuel

Look right, not left

The possessive agrees with the thing owned — never with the owner. This is the opposite of English.

3 En application

The two traps

Same owner, different objects

Paul aime son chien et sa maison.

One owner, but the word changes because the nouns differ.

Different owners, same object

Paul aime sa maison. Marie aime sa maison.

Two owners, but the word is identical.

⚠️ MON before a feminine vowel

mon amie · mon école · ton histoire

Not ma amie. Before a vowel or mute h, feminine nouns take mon/ton/son for sound.

⚠️ LEUR vs LEURS

leur maison · leurs maisons

Several owners, but still count the object: one house → leur, many houses → leurs.
