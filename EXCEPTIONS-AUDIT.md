## The one thing added

**Slide `pc-4`** (Le Passé Composé → *Pièges*), appended as a new `.box` after the existing decision routine. Nothing on the slide was rewritten or reordered.

It carries a purple **"Enrichment — beyond the core Class 10 syllabus"** pill (`.stretch-note`), so you can skip it with a weaker class without hunting for it.

Content:

> With *avoir*, the participle normally agrees with a direct object placed **before** the verb. But a few verbs — *coûter, valoir, peser, courir, vivre* — used in their **measure** sense are followed by a quantity, not a true direct object, so they stay invariable.
>
> - Les 200 euros que ce livre a **coûté**. — not *coûtés*; 200 euros is a price, not a COD.
> - Les trois heures que j'ai **couru**. — not *courues*; three hours is a duration.
> - Used figuratively they take a real COD and **do** agree: *Les efforts que ce travail m'a coûtés.*

### Teacher's eye, please

This is the one item worth your review before it reaches students, for two reasons:

1. **It is genuinely above Class 10 level.** It appears in CBSE material only rarely. It is tagged as enrichment for exactly that reason — delete the box if you would rather not open this door.
2. **The figurative contrast is subtle.** *Les efforts que ce travail m'a coûtés* agrees because figurative *coûter* is transitive, while *les 200 euros que ce livre a coûté* does not. Strong students find this fascinating; weaker ones may over-generalise and start making participles invariable everywhere. Your call whether the last line stays.

Everything else in this pass was verification only — no other grammar content was changed.
