Le système éducatif en Inde est caractérisé par le modèle 10 + 2 + 3. L’enseignement est public,

l’enseignement secondaire supérieur à la fin de ces études. Après cela, on peut faire 3 ans dans 
un « College of Arts and Science » ou 4 ou 5 ans dans une école professionnelle après avoir

Kuwait, March 31, 2026 
 
Dear Marie, 
How are you? I am doing well here. I received your letter in which you asked me for 
information about the education system in India. Schooling in India is a bit different from that 
in France. 
 
The education system in India is characterized by the 10 + 2 + 3  model. Education is public, 
free, and compulsory until the age of 14. Children are enrolled in school at the age of 3. They 
spend 10 years in "High School." After passing the exam, they receive the CBSE certificate. 
Then, they spend 2 years in "Higher Secondary School." They obtain the Higher Secondary 
diploma at the end of these studies. After that, one can study for 3 years in a "College of Arts 
and Science" or for 4 or 5 years in a professional school  after passing a competitive entrance 
exam. 
 
Say hello to your parents for me. Write to me often. 
 
Your friend,  
XYZ
