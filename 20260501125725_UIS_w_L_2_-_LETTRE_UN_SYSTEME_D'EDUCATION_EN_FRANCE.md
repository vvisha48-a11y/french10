Dear Rahul, 
How are you? I hope everyone is fine there. I am writing to describe the education system in

Here, school is free and compulsory from the age of 2 to 16. The system is divided into three 
levels. Firstly, one enrols in kindergarten from 2 to 6 years, then in primary school from 6 to 
11 years. Next, they go to middle school from 11 to 15 years to obtain the Brevet. After that, 
they enter high school for three years: Seconde, Première, and Terminale. At the end of 
Terminale, students take the Baccalaureate exam. This diploma is essential to enter university. 
After the Bac, students can continue their studies at university or choose vocational training.
