1 What a Message Is

What a Message Is

5marks, every year
30–50words is plenty
4parts, always the same
~8minutes in the exam
  • ✉️A message is a short note — the kind you leave on a table or send quickly. Not a letter.
  • 🎯The examiner checks four things: is it laid out right, does it answer the task, is the French correct, is the vocabulary right.
  • 🔁The situation changes every year. The skeleton never does.

💡 Why this is the easiest 5 marks on the paper

You can write the date, the salutation and the sign-off before you even read the question. That is roughly a third of the message, already correct.

2 The Message Box

The Message Box

💡 The box is the mark

Miss the date line or the signature and you lose the format mark even if every French word is perfect. Draw the four slots first, fill them second.

Place, Date and Time

Header› Date / Time› Message› Sign-off
✓ Write this✗ Not this
Delhi, le 28 novembreDelhi, Le 28 Novembre
Months never take a capital in French, and le is lowercase too.

The comma between the town and the date is not optional.
Paris, 14 hParis, 14h00
A space before h, and nothing after it. Never 2 pm.
le 1er maile 1 mai
Only the first of the month is ordinal. All others are plain: le 2 mai.

⚠️ The one that catches everybody

If the task says the person goes out or leaves a note, write the time, not the date. A note on the kitchen table is timed, not dated.

💡 Three sentences is a full answer

One sentence per move. A 40-word message that does all three beats an 80-word one that only does the first.

⚠️ Two lines, not one

The closing and the signature are separate lines. Ton amie then Avaanika underneath. Running them together loses the format mark.

💡 Write the skeleton first

Put down the four slots the moment you read the question. Even if you then run out of time, the format mark is already banked.

1 The Three Types

The Three Types

✉️Invitation — you ask
✅Acceptance — you say yes
🙏Refusal — you say no, kindly
  • 🧩All three use the same four-part skeleton. Only the middle changes.
  • ⏱️Decide the type in the first ten seconds. The verb in the question tells you: invitez, acceptez, refusez.

⚠️ The costliest misread

Answering an invitation when the paper asked for a refusal loses the content marks outright. Underline the instruction verb before you write a word.

7 Where the 5 Marks Are

Where the 5 Marks Are

Click any line to see which of the five marks it earns.

Format mark. Town, comma, le, day, lowercase month. Four things to get right in five words.

Format mark. Chère agrees with Nivya, and the comma is compulsory.

Content mark. Why + when + where in one sentence. This is the sentence the task actually asked for.

Grammar mark. Future serai plus the feminine agreement contente, because Avaanika is writing.

Format mark. On its own line, above the name — and feminine again.

Format mark. The signature is the last line. Nothing goes under it.

    Practice — Acceptance

    Votre ami(e) vous invite à sa fête. Vous acceptez et vous proposez d'apporter quelque chose. (30 mots)

    Your friend invites you to their party. You accept and offer to bring something. (30 words)

    Show a model answer

    Chennai, le 14 avril

    Chère Anjali,

    💡 The move most students forget

    Offer something. A message that only says yes is thin; one that offers to bring the cake answers the whole task.

    4 Practice — Refusal

    Practice — Refusal

    Practice prompt · drafted for review

    Votre ami(e) vous invite à un concert, mais vous ne pouvez pas y aller. Refusez et donnez une raison. (30 mots)

    Your friend invites you to a concert, but you cannot go. Refuse and give a reason. (30 words)

    Show a model answer

    Chennai, le 18 avril

    Cher Rahul,

    Je te remercie de ton invitation, mais je suis désolée, je ne peux pas venir au concert.

    Il faut que j'aille chez ma grand-mère ce week-end. Nous sortirons une autre fois !

    Ton amie

    Meera

    💡 Three moves, three marks

    Thank · refuse · explain. The model adds a fourth — offer another time — which is what turns a good answer into a full one.

    Mark It Yourself

    Delhi, le 30 mai

    Chère Deepti,

    Merci pour ton invitation. Je suis désolée, je ne peux pas venir.

    Ton amie

    Priya

    Award the 5 marks yourself. Then open each row to see the examiner's split.

    1 / 1 All four slots are there and on their own lines.

    1 / 2 It refuses and thanks, but gives no reason — and the task said donnez une raison.

    1 / 1 Future and a correct feminine agreement on désolée.

    0 / 1 Je suis busy — an English word in a French answer scores nothing.

    ⚠️ 3 out of 5, and the French was almost perfect

    Two marks went for things that had nothing to do with grammar: a missing reason and one English word. That is where messages are usually lost.

    4 Tricks for the Full 5

    Tricks for the Full 5

    • 1️⃣Draw the four slots before you write. The format mark is banked in ten seconds.
    • 2️⃣Underline the instruction verb — invitez / acceptez / refusez. Answering the wrong type costs the most marks.
    • 3️⃣Check your own gender on désolé(e), content(e), ton ami(e). Free marks, endlessly thrown away.
    • 4️⃣If the task says donnez une raison, give one. It is a separate mark.
    • 5️⃣Count your words. 30–50. Longer is not better — it just gives you more chances to make a mistake.
    • 6️⃣Slip in one subjunctive or one relative pronoun. That is the grammar mark, deliberately earned.
    5 Before You Hand It In

    Before You Hand It In

    • ☑️Town, comma, le, day, lowercase month — or the time if it is a note.
    • ☑️Cher / Chère matches the reader, and there is a comma.
    • ☑️The task's verb is answered — invited, accepted, or refused with a reason.
    • ☑️Every -é has its accent; no English words anywhere.
    • ☑️Closing and signature on two separate lines.

    💡 Thirty seconds, one mark

    Read it back once, looking only at capital letters and accents. That single pass is usually worth a mark on its own.
