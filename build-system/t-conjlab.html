👨‍👩‍👧 Verb Families — learn one, get the whole group

French verbs travel in families. Learn the pattern once and every verb in that family follows it.

Regular

Regular -ER
parler, manger, acheter, appeler — the biggest family by far

Regular -IR
finir, choisir — adds -iss- in the plural

Regular -RE
vendre, attendre — il/elle takes no ending

Pattern families

-IR like partir
partir, dormir, sortir — no -iss-: je pars, nous partons

Prendre family
prendre, comprendre, apprendre — ils prennent

Venir / Tenir
venir, tenir — je viens, futur stem viendr-

The tricky ones

Boot verbs
boire, devoir, pouvoir, vouloir — the stem changes in nous / vous only

Essential irregulars
être, avoir, aller, faire, voir — must be memorised

🥾 Why "boot verbs"? Write the six forms in a column and draw a line around the ones that share the irregular stem — je / tu / il and ils sit inside; nous and vous fall outside. The shape looks like a boot.
