1 Usages
Where you actually meet it

Why pronouns matter — French hates repetition

Photo: Unsplash
🗣️ In conversation

— Tu aimes ce film ? — Oui, je l'adore !

Repeating ce film would sound clumsy.

✉️ In a letter or email

J'ai reçu ta lettre et je t'écris tout de suite.

I received your letter and I am writing to you right away.

🛍️ Shopping

— Vous voulez des pommes ? — Oui, j'en veux un kilo.

EN replaces de + noun and keeps the quantity.

📝 In the CBSE exam

Remplacez les mots soulignés par un pronom.

The classic board instruction — spot COD vs COI first.

2 Mémo visuel

The train — carriages always run in this order

Pronouns are carriages coupled in front of the engine. The engine is the verb. You can never re-order the carriages.

3 En application

Two carriages at once

COD + COI together

Il donne le livre à Marie. → Il le lui donne.

Carriage 2 (le) then carriage 3 (lui) — never the reverse.

With EN

Il me parle de ses vacances. → Il m'en parle.

EN is always the last carriage before the verb.

⚠️ The affirmative imperative flips the train

Donne-le-moi !

Here only, pronouns go after the verb with hyphens, and me becomes moi.

⚠️ The negative imperative is normal again

Ne me le donne pas !

The train goes back in front of the verb.
