1 Usages
Where you actually meet it

La négation in the real world

Photo: Unsplash
🛒 At the market

Désolé, je n'ai plus de tomates.

Sorry, I have no more tomatoes.

🚫 On public signs

Ne pas marcher sur la pelouse.

Do not walk on the grass. — infinitive form on signs.

💬 Turning down an invitation

Je ne peux pas venir, je suis occupé.

I can't come, I'm busy.

📝 In the CBSE exam

Mettez à la forme négative : « Elle mange de la viande. »

A standard board question — watch the partitive!

2 Mémo visuel

The sandwich — ne … pas wraps the verb

The subject stays outside. The complement stays outside. Only the conjugated verb gets eaten.

Compound tenses: the sandwich closes around the auxiliary, not the participle — Je n'ai pas parlé. The past participle sits outside the bread.

3 En application

Reading négation in the wild

🍽️ On a café menu

Ce plat ne contient pas de gluten.

This dish contains no gluten. — de, never du, after a negation.

🚉 At the station

Le train n'est pas encore arrivé.

The train hasn't arrived yet.

📱 In a text message

J'ai rien compris !

Spoken French drops ne. Never do this in the exam — write Je n'ai rien compris.

📖 In a story

Elle ne voyait personne dans la rue.

She saw nobody in the street.
