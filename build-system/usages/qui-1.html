1a Usages · Décrire qui fait l'action
Où on l'utilise vraiment

QUI est le sujet — un verbe le suit toujours

QUI joins two sentences when the repeated word was the one doing the action. A verb always comes straight after it.

C'est le train qui part à huit heures.

It is the train that leaves at eight o'clock.

⚠️ QUI ne s'élide jamais. Even before a vowel it keeps its i.

✓ la femme qui habite ici  ·  ✗ la femme qu'habite ici

Works for people and things alike, and the participle never agrees after QUI.
