1a Usages · Ce qui a changé
Où on l'utilise vraiment

Avant oui — maintenant non

Something used to be true and has stopped. English: "no longer", "not any more".

Il n'habite plus à Paris.

He no longer lives in Paris.

⚠️ PLUS a deux vies. Without ne it means "more" — the opposite meaning.

Je veux plus de sucre. ← more sugar
Je ne veux plus de sucre. ← no more sugar

The little word ne is what flips it. Never drop it in writing.
