1a Usages · Intentions & décisions
Usage 1 sur 2

C'est décidé — on le fait bientôt

A plan that is already settled and close at hand. The decision is made — you are simply announcing it.

Nous allons déménager en juillet.

We're going to move house in July.

The words that go with it: tout de suite dans cinq minutes ce soir demain bientôt

Anything further away than that, and French reaches for the futur simple instead.

1b Usages · Preuve sous les yeux
Usage 2 sur 2

On le voit venir — la preuve est là

Something you can see coming right now. The evidence is in front of you, so the future is a step away — this is where English says "it's going to…", never "it will".

Attention, tu vas tomber !

Careful, you're going to fall!

Compare:

Il va pleuvoir. ← les nuages sont déjà là
Il pleuvra demain. ← la météo l'annonce pour demain

Same weather, two different distances.
