⏳ Past · Present · Future — at a glance

The same ten tenses, grouped by time. This is your quick revision map.

⏪ THE PAST

Plus-que-parfait
j'avais mangé — I had eaten

Imparfait
je mangeais — I was eating / used to

Passé Composé
j'ai mangé — I ate / have eaten

Passé Récent
je viens de manger — I have just eaten

⏺ THE PRESENT

Présent
je mange — I eat / am eating

Conditionnel (mood)
je mangerais — I would eat

Subjonctif (mood)
que je mange — (that) I eat

⏩ THE FUTURE

Futur Proche
je vais manger — I am going to eat

Futur Simple
je mangerai — I will eat

Futur Antérieur
j'aurai mangé — I will have eaten

🔑 The one pattern that unlocks four tenses: the compound tenses are just auxiliary (être/avoir) + past participle. Change only the auxiliary's tense: présent → Passé Composé · imparfait → Plus-que-parfait · futur → Futur Antérieur.
