What "tu" changes

3 Mistakes that cost marks

Mistakes that cost marks

  • ⛔Mixing tu and vous — by far the most common
  • ⛔Capital on the month — le 13 Octobre
  • ⛔Forgetting the town before the date
  • ⛔Cher before a girl’s name
  • ⛔Signing Cordialement in a friendly letter
  • ⛔Writing about the wrong thing — the most expensive of all

⚠️ The drift

The register mistake rarely happens at the start. You begin with tu, drift into vous in paragraph 2, and never notice. Check every verb before you hand in.

Paris, 22 May 2021

Chère Marie,

Dans cette lettre, je vais te décrire l'enseignement français. L'enseignement en France est un peu différent de celui en Inde. Il est aussi gratuit, public et obligatoire jusqu'à 16 ans. L'enseignement est divisé en trois degrés : primaire, secondaire et supérieur.

In this letter I will describe French education to you. Teaching in France is a little different from teaching in India. It is also free, public and compulsory up to 16 years old. Education is divided into three levels: primary, secondary and higher.

Au degré primaire, l'enfant peut commencer sa scolarité à l'âge de 2 ans à 6 ans avec l'école maternelle. Ensuite, l'école primaire à l'âge de 6 ans à 10 ans. À l'enseignement secondaire, l'enfant entre au collège de 11 ans à 15 ans et puis au lycée à l'âge de 15 ans à 18 ans.

At primary level, a child can start school between the ages of 2 and 6 with nursery school. Then comes primary school, from 6 to 10 years old. In secondary education, the child goes to middle school from 11 to 15, and then to high school from 15 to 18.

Au lycée, les trois années s'appellent la Seconde, la Première et la Terminale.

At high school, the three years are called Seconde, Première and Terminale.

On obtient le diplôme du Brevet pour le collège et le Baccalauréat pour le lycée. À la fin, l'enseignement supérieur est l'université. Après le Bac, on peut choisir la formation professionnelle.

You get the Brevet diploma for middle school and the Baccalaureate for high school. Finally, higher education is university. After the Bac, you can choose vocational training.

Et toi, quel est le système en Inde ? J'attends ta réponse.

And you, what is the system like in India? I am waiting for your reply.

Your friend,

The French President is elected by the citizens for five years. Located in the heart of Paris, near the Champs-Élysées, the Élysée Palace is where the President works and lives. The President of the French Republic is called Emmanuel Macron.

The President of the French Republic has many responsibilities: he represents the whole country. When he travels officially abroad, he speaks in the name of France and of all French people.
