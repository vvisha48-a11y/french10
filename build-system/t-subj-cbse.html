1 What is the subjunctive?

A verb form used after que

It is a verb form (a mood) used after que to show a wish, a need, a feeling, or a doubt.

A wish

Je veux que…

A need

Il faut que…

A feeling

Je suis content que…

A doubt

Je doute que…

1 What is the subjunctive?

Look at the examples

Il fauta need
queque
tuwho?
travailles.subjunctive

(You must work.)

Je veuxa wish
queque
vouswho?
veniez.subjunctive

(I want you to come.)

Rule to remember

The subjunctive almost always comes after que.

Good news

For -ER verbs, je, tu, il and ils look exactly like the present tense. Only nous and vous are new.

5 Two-stem verbs

"Boot verbs"

Some verbs have two stems in the subjunctive

Stem 1

From the ils form (remove -ent)

ils boivent → boiv-

Used with je, tu, il/elle, ils/elles

Stem 2

From the nous form (remove -ons)

nous buvons → buv-

Used with nous, vous

Why?

These verbs already change in the nous and vous forms of the present tense (ils boivent but nous buvons). The subjunctive keeps the same change.

5 Two-stem verbs

Picture it: the boot

je, tu, il and ils sit inside the "boot". Nous and vous stay outside.

Shortcut

The nous and vous forms of the subjunctive are the same as the imparfait: nous buvions, vous buviez.

5 Two-stem verbs

Watch out: y + i

Keep both letters

nousvoyions
nouscroyions
nouspayions
Why both?

The y belongs to the stem (voy-), and the i belongs to the ending (-ions). Nothing is dropped.

7 When to use the subjunctive

Same person or different person?

Same person?

Use the infinitive

Je veux partir.

Different person?

Use the subjunctive

Je veux que tu partes.

8 Memory tricks

Memory tricks (1–4)

1. Always que in front.

Il faut que tu travailles.

2. The endings never change

-e-es-e-ions-iez-ent

The only exceptions are être and avoir.

3. je, tu, il and ils sound the same

boive · boives · boive · boivent

…because their endings are silent.

4. -ER verbs: je, tu, il, ils

que je parle = je parle

The same as the present tense.

8 Memory tricks

Memory tricks (5–7)

5. Nous and vous = the imparfait

que nous buvions = nous buvions

…except for être, avoir, faire, pouvoir and savoir.

6. y + i: keep both letters

voyions · croyions

7. Only 7 verbs are truly special

êtreavoirallerfairepouvoirsavoirvouloir
