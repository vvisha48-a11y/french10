⏪ LE PASSÉ

Plus-que-parfait
j'avais parlé — had spoken

Imparfait
je parlais — was speaking / used to

Passé composé
j'ai parlé — spoke / have spoken

⏺ LE PRÉSENT

Présent
je parle — I speak

Impératif
Parle ! — Speak!

Conditionnel
je parlerais — I would speak

⏩ LE FUTUR

Futur proche
je vais parler — am going to speak

Futur simple
je parlerai — will speak

Futur antérieur
j'aurai parlé — will have spoken

🔑 The pattern that unlocks four tenses: compound tenses are just auxiliaire + participe passé. Change only the auxiliary's tense: présent → passé composé · imparfait → plus-que-parfait · futur → futur antérieur · conditionnel → conditionnel passé.

✅ Check-list — before you hand in the paper

Run through these six checks on every verb you write. Most lost marks are here, not in the rules.

☐ Which group? Check the nous form — finissons or partons?

☐ Which tense does the sentence signal? hier → passé composé · souvent → imparfait · demain → futur

☐ Compound tense? Then ask: avoir or être?

☐ If être — did you make the participle agree with the subject?

☐ Negation — does ne … pas wrap the auxiliary, not the participle?

☐ Accents — é in parlé, è in achète. A missing accent is a missing mark.

🎯 Remember the weighting: verbs are the one compulsory question — 5 marks guaranteed to appear. You then choose 5 of the remaining 6 topics. Securing verbs protects a sixth of your whole grammar section before you start.
