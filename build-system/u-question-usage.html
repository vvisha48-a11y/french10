3 En application

Three registers, one meaning

🗣️ Familier — intonation only

Tu viens ?

Spoken French. Fine with friends, risky in the exam.

✅ Standard — est-ce que

Est-ce que tu viens ?

Always correct. Word order never changes after it.

🎩 Formel — l'inversion

Viens-tu ?

Hyphen required. Add -t- between two vowels: A-t-il fini ?

⚠️ QUI vs QUE at the start

Qui vient ? ← the person doing it
Qu'est-ce que tu fais ? ← the thing being done

QUI asks for the subject, QUE asks for the object.
